Games from the textbook "Game Theory through Examples"
By Erich Prisner, MAA (2012), Original website: eprisner.de

Lisa's Game
A variant of the classic game of Nim (rules by Prof. Erich Prisner's daughter, Lisa).
Nim
The classic heap-based game that started combinatorial game theory.
Quatro-Uno
A card elimination game.
Auction Game
A two-player painting auction game.
Five Knights
A voting game exploring Nash equilibria among five self-interested knights.
Five Exhausted Knights
A variant of Five Knights where an unresolved election actively hurts everyone.
Five Knights (Random)
Five Knights with randomized starting preferences, including a "big-ego" mode.
Simultaneous Quatro-Uno
Blindly commit your card order against the computer's optimal mixed strategy.
Simultaneous Quatro-Uno (2P)
The blind pile-ordering version of Quatro-Uno, played hotseat between two people.
NIM(n)
Take one or two coins from a single row — a two-player hotseat classic.
NIM(n) vs Computer
Take one or two stones from a row against a computer playing the optimal mod-3 strategy.
