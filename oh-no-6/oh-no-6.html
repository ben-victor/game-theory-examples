Oh-No 6

Rules: Same game as Oh-No, with one twist: roll a die as many times as you like, and after each roll that many dollars are added to your heap, which starts empty.
  • You may stop anytime and keep whatever's in the heap.
  • Roll a 6 and the entire heap is lost — instantly, no matter how big it was.
