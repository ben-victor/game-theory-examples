1-Candidate Quiz Show

Rules: You play a quiz show with up to six questions. Answering a question correctly puts a certain (increasing) amount of money on the board. After each round you may either stop and keep everything accumulated so far, or hear the next question. Once you've heard a question, there's no backing out — you must answer it, and a wrong answer loses everything you've built up.

The worth of each question and your probability of getting it wrong are known to you from the start — these are the "parameters" of the game, and you can edit them below to try different versions.
