Simultaneous Quatro-Uno (Two Players)

Rules: Ann and Beth each have the cards 1, 2, 3, 4, and each secretly arranges their four cards into a pile, front to back — a single blind decision, not something either can react to.
  • Each round, both piles reveal their front card.
  • The higher card wins and stays at the front to guard again next round; the lower one is discarded.
  • Equal cards are both discarded.
  • Special rule: a 1 facing a 4 also discards both, even though 4 is higher.
Whoever's pile empties first loses.

This is a two-player, take-turns-on-one-screen tool — like the original applet, it works best if Ann and Beth each decide their order privately (away from the screen, or written down) before one of you enters both piles below, so neither sees the other's order in advance.
