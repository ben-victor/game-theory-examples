5 Envelopes

Rules: There are 5 envelopes. One always contains nothing; the other four contain increasing amounts of money. The envelopes are shuffled, and each round you may either ask for one more (randomly chosen from whatever's left) or stop and keep what you've accumulated. Draw the empty envelope and you lose everything you've collected so far — otherwise its value is added to your total.
Parameters

Envelope 1 is always empty. Set the other four amounts, then press Apply — this also resets the average-win tracker below for the new parameter set.
