3 × 3 Rec the Square

Rec the Square

White and Black alternately place dominoes (two orthogonally adjacent squares) onto the 3×3 board — either player can place a domino in any orientation. Click an empty square to tentatively start a domino there; click it again to cancel; or click an empty square adjacent to it to complete and confirm the domino. Whoever cannot fit a new domino in loses — the other player wins.

Creating Holes

A different game uses the exact same moves, but scores it differently: play continues (each player who can move must move) until someone can't move at all. Then White earns $1 for every empty corner square left over, and Black earns $1 for every other empty square (edges and the center). Nobody "wins" outright — it's about maximizing your own payoff. Every game you play here is scored both ways at the end, so you can compare the two.
