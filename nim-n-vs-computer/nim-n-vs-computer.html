NIM(n) vs Computer

Rules: NIM(n) is played by two players, who alternate removing one or two stones from a single row of originally n stones. Only the stones at the right-hand end are ever available — tap the last stone to remove one, or the second-to-last stone to remove that one along with the last. Whoever cannot move because no stones are left loses (equivalently: whoever takes the last stone wins). You move first, and the row can start with anywhere from 4 to 11 stones.

The computer plays optimally whenever it can: it always leaves a multiple of 3 stones behind, which is a guaranteed win with correct play. If it's ever forced to move from a multiple of 3 itself, the position is already lost regardless of what it does, so it picks between one and two at random.
