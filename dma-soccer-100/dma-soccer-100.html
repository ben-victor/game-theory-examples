100 Rounds in DMA Soccer

DMA Soccer: The field has three zones: Left, Midfield, and Right. Two teams, Blue and Red, each send 5 players onto the field, split across Defense / Midfield / Attack — and each player stays in their zone the whole match. The ball starts at midfield after kickoff and after every goal.
  • If a Blue midfielder gets the ball, they throw it into the right zone. A Blue attacker there scores a goal; a Red defender there throws it back to midfield instead.
  • Symmetrically, a Red midfielder throws left: a Red attacker scores, a Blue defender sends it back to midfield.
  • The ball is never thrown directly between the left and right zones.
Before starting, arrange each team's formation with the arrows below (always 5 players, shuffled between adjacent zones). Once you hit Play, formations lock for the rest of that match — this simulator runs the requested number of rounds and tallies results, then lets you tweak formations and run it again.
