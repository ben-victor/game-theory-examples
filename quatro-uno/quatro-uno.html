Quatro-Uno

Rules: Both players start with cards 1, 2, 3, 4 in their hand. On each play, both players select one of their own cards. The two selections are compared.

The player whose selected card has the higher value wins the round, and that card returns to their hand. The losing player discards the card they played.

In the event of a tie, both cards are discarded.

Special rule: When 1 faces 4, both cards are discarded.

The first player to discard all of their cards loses the game.

The game ends in a draw if both players discard their last card at the same time.

Turns alternate between the player and the computer.
