Nim

Nim is one of the oldest known combinatorial games. Its name is due to Charles L. Bouton, who formally analyzed and developed a complete theory of the game in the early 1900s.

Rules: There are several heaps of tokens. On your turn, pick one heap and remove one or more tokens from it — you can't touch any other heap that turn.

Whoever removes the last token wins.

Turns alternate between the player and the computer.

Tap a token to preview a move: it selects that token and every token after it in the same heap. Confirm to remove them.
