NIM(n)

Rules: NIM(n) is played by two players, White and Black, who alternate turns removing one or two coins from a single row of n coins. Only the coins at the right-hand end of the row are ever available to take — tap the last coin to remove one, or the second-to-last coin to remove that one along with the last. Whoever cannot move because no coins are left loses (equivalently: whoever takes the last coin wins). The row can start with anywhere from 5 to 10 coins.
