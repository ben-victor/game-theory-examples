Auction

Rules: Six paintings, worth $1500, $2500, $3500, $4500, $5500, and $6500, are auctioned one at a time in a random order unknown to the bidders. Each starts at $1000, and the price rises $1000 per round as Ann and Beth alternate responding "Yes" (raise / stay in) or "Pass" (drop out). If both pass before anyone bids, the painting goes unsold. A bidder's final payoff is the value of paintings they won minus the money they spent — bidders don't care how the other player does, only about their own payoff.

This is a two-player, take-turns-on-one-screen game — hand the device back and forth between Ann and Beth.
