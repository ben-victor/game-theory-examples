Polyomino Rec-the-Square
with Randomness (4×4)

Rules: The same domino-placing game as the 3×3 and 3×4 random versions, now on a 4×4 board. White and Black alternately place dominoes (two adjacent squares, any orientation), and whoever cannot fit a new domino in loses.

The polyomino rule still applies: every square placed — by either player, or by chance — must stay connected to the shape already on the board (touching it edge-to-edge). The very first move of the game is exempt, since there's nothing to connect to yet. After each completed domino, a red square is thrown onto a random empty square that still satisfies the polyomino rule — shrinking the board for both players without belonging to either of them.

Click an empty, validly-connected square to tentatively start a domino there; click it again to cancel; or click an adjacent empty square to complete and confirm it.
